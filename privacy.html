Ocean Code Sudoku

Privacy notice

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Pre-release notice. Ocean Code Sudoku (Android, package com.oceancodestudio.sudoku) is in testing and not yet publicly released. This notice describes the build being tested. We will review it again against the released version.

Who we are

Ocean Code Studio (“we”) develops Ocean Code Sudoku. For support or privacy requests, contact kamarwessam@gmail.com. This notice applies to the game and this website.

Game data on your device

The game saves your puzzle, settings, statistics, recently played puzzle numbers, hint balance, ad-frequency counters and a record that Pro was verified in app-specific storage. The Pro record includes a one-way hash of the Google Play purchase token; the game does not write the original token to its save files. We have no app account system, cloud-save service or developer-operated game server, and we do not receive these save files.

The app opts out of Android cloud backup. Some device manufacturers may still transfer app data when you move to a new phone. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes the local saves on that device; copies made by device-transfer tools may need separate removal. An eligible Pro purchase can be restored through the Google Play account that bought it, which requires connecting to Google Play.

Unity Analytics and Unity Crash Reporting are disabled, and we do not run a separate gameplay analytics service. Advertising services in the free version still process measurement and diagnostic information as described below.

Google Play: Pro purchase and optional puzzle downloads

The planned Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. Purchasing is not yet publicly available during this testing period. Google processes payments; the game does not receive your full card or bank details. The app checks ownership with Google Play to unlock and restore Pro, and remembers a verified purchase so Pro also works offline. Google Play Billing may send its own diagnostic information to Google.

Additional puzzle packs are optional downloads delivered by Google Play (Play Asset Delivery). See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising in the free version only

The free version can show optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one for an extra hint) and occasional full-screen ads between finished puzzles — never during a puzzle. There are no banner ads. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource Mobile) mediation, with Unity Ads and Google AdMob as ad sources.

When advertising services are used, these providers may process advertising and other device identifiers, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and app information, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic data. They use this information to deliver and measure ads, assess ad quality, limit repetition and prevent fraud. LevelPlay includes its Ad Quality service. What providers process depends on your region, privacy choices and the service configuration. See Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (Unity Ads and LevelPlay/ironSource) and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Pro is completely ad-free. When Pro is known on your device, the game does not start any advertising or ad-consent service at all, and Pro hints never require watching an ad.

Your privacy choices

In the free version, the game uses Google’s consent tool (User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the tool presents the applicable choices before allowing ad requests. In supported US states, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising; the game passes that choice to its advertising services. Choosing not to consent does not prevent you from playing Sudoku.

You can review or change your choices at any time in Settings › Privacy › Privacy choices (shown where these rules apply). Android also lets you reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings. Refusing personalised ads does not remove all technical processing needed to deliver an ad.

Intended audience

The intended audience is people aged 13 and over; the game is not directed at children under 13. Additional regional protections can apply to teenagers. Age-related advertising protections are still being reviewed before public release. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through the game.

Support, retention and your rights

If you email us, we receive your address and anything you include, and use it only to answer you. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request. Please do not send sensitive information.

We do not hold your game saves, so we cannot see, export or delete them for you; you control local saves through the app’s storage settings. Uninstalling the game does not delete Google Play purchase history or records held by advertising providers. Google, Unity and ironSource have their own retention rules and privacy controls, linked above. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information; email us about anything we hold or for help directing a request.

This website and changes

These pages use no analytics, trackers, external fonts or cookies. The host (GitHub Pages) may process standard request information such as IP addresses; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We will update this page, and its date, when the app’s services or practices change.
