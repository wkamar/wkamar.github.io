Privacy notice

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Pre-release notice. Ocean Code Sudoku (Android, package com.oceancodestudio.sudoku) is in testing and not yet publicly released. This notice describes version 2.0.0, build 203, prepared for internal testing. Test builds disable ordinary ad requests; clearly labelled test ads may be used to check the integration. We will review this notice again before public release.

Who we are

Ocean Code Studio (“we”) develops Ocean Code Sudoku. For support or privacy requests, contact kamarwessam@gmail.com. This notice applies to the game and this website.

Game data on your device

The game saves your puzzle, settings, statistics, recently played puzzle numbers, which of the free puzzles you have completed, hint balance, ad-frequency counters and a record that Pro was verified in app-specific storage. The Pro record includes a one-way hash of the Google Play purchase token; the game does not write the original token to its save files. If you are asked the age question described below, only your answer band (for example “16 or older”) is saved, not a birth date. We have no app account system, cloud-save service or developer-operated game server, and we do not receive these files.

Google’s consent tool stores your advertising privacy choices on the device in the standard IAB format so that the ad services can read them. The app opts out of Android cloud backup. Some device manufacturers may still transfer app data when you move to a new phone. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes this data from that device; copies made by device-transfer tools may need separate removal. An eligible Pro purchase can be restored through the Google Play account that bought it, which requires connecting to Google Play.

Unity Analytics and Unity Crash Reporting are disabled, and we do not run a separate gameplay analytics service. Advertising services in the free version still process measurement and diagnostic information as described below.

Google Play: Pro purchase and optional puzzle downloads

The free version includes 1,000 puzzles (100 at each of the ten levels). Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing that unlocks the full library of 10 million puzzles and removes all ads. Google processes payments; the game does not receive your full card or bank details. The app checks ownership with Google Play to unlock and restore Pro, and remembers a verified purchase so Pro also works offline. Google Play Billing may send its own diagnostic information to Google.

With Pro, additional puzzle packs are optional downloads delivered by Google Play (Play Asset Delivery); nothing is downloaded unless you choose it. App reviewers can be given a review code that unlocks the same Pro features on their device; it is not a purchase and nothing about it is sent to us. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising in the free version only

The free version can show optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one for an extra hint) and occasional full-screen ads between finished puzzles — never during a puzzle. There are no banner ads. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay mediation (operated by ironSource Mobile, part of Unity), with Unity Ads and Google AdMob as ad sources. ironSource exchange demand is disabled. LevelPlay and its Ad Quality service remain part of the integration.

No advertising service starts until the game has confirmed, in the current session, that ads are allowed for you (see “Your privacy choices”). Until then — for example while you are offline or if the check fails — the game shows no ads and you can keep playing. When advertising services do run, these providers may process advertising and other device identifiers, pseudonymous user identifiers, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and app information, ad views and interactions, purchase history and diagnostic data. Their purposes include delivering and measuring ads, app and SDK functionality, assessing ad quality, limiting repetition and preventing fraud. Unity’s SDK disclosure includes user identifiers and purchase history even though this game has no account system and does not send its local save files. What providers process depends on your region and choices. See Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (Unity Ads, LevelPlay and ironSource) and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Google ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences, and Unity Ads is set to its strictest app/game rating filter, to match the game’s content rating. Pro is completely ad-free. When Pro is known on your device, the game does not start any advertising or ad-consent service at all, and Pro hints never require watching an ad.

Your privacy choices

In the free version the game uses Google’s consent tool (User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform) before any advertising service starts. The consent tool itself contacts Google to find out which rules apply where you are and to load the right message. Its requests include technical app and device details, language and stored privacy choices; Google also receives the connection’s IP address. This happens before your advertising eligibility is known. We do not use cross-app consent syncing or send Google an email-based consent identifier.

Age for advertising, everywhere. At a menu screen the game asks whether you are under 16 or 16 and older. This is our conservative launch rule for advertising, not a statement of the legal age in every country. If you answer under 16, or leave the question unanswered, advertising stays off and every free puzzle remains available. An answer does not become permission merely because you travel to another region. The consent tool may already have contacted Google to establish which privacy rules apply.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. For eligible players, Google’s consent message explains the purposes and lists the partners, and you can consent, refuse or choose in detail. It appears at a menu, never during a puzzle. Ads run only when the required device-storage and partner permissions are present for the services used (Google, Unity Ads and ironSource Mobile’s LevelPlay mediation). If you refuse required permissions, the game shows no ads and starts no advertising service; every free puzzle stays available and the consent form is not repeatedly demanded at each launch. Your choices are passed to the advertising services through the consent tool’s standard signals.

Protective ad settings for teens and adults. Our age question groups everyone aged 16 and older together. This release therefore applies Google’s teen advertising treatment and Unity’s non-behavioural advertising setting to every eligible player, including adults. Giving consent does not turn on personalised ads in this release. These settings still permit the technical data processing needed to deliver, measure and protect advertising.

You can change your age answer in Settings › Privacy › Age for ads. Where Google’s consent tool requires a privacy entry point, Privacy choices lets you change those choices. Opening privacy choices pauses new ad requests; previous cached ads are discarded when the decision changes. If you withdraw required permissions, the game stops loading and showing ads. Advertising services that had already started cannot be unloaded from the running process, but receive updated choices; a later launch starts them only after eligibility is established again.

US states with privacy laws. You can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising in Settings › Privacy › Privacy choices; the game passes that choice to its advertising services immediately.

Everywhere. Android lets you reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings. Non-personalised ads still require some technical processing, such as your IP address, to deliver and measure an ad and prevent fraud.

Intended audience

The intended audience is people aged 13 and over; the game is not directed at children under 13. The advertising age question applies in every region; players below the advertising threshold can still use the free game without ads. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through the game.

Support, retention and your rights

If you email us, we receive your address and anything you include, and use it only to answer you. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request. Please do not send sensitive information.

We do not hold your game saves, so we cannot see, export or delete them for you; you control local saves through the app’s storage settings. Uninstalling the game does not delete Google Play purchase history or records held by advertising providers. Google, Unity and ironSource have their own retention rules and privacy controls, linked above. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information; email us about anything we hold or for help directing a request.

This website and changes

These pages use no analytics, trackers, external fonts or cookies. The host (GitHub Pages) may process standard request information such as IP addresses; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We will update this page, and its date, when the app’s services or practices change.
