Ocean Code Sudoku

Privacy notice

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Pre-release notice. Ocean Code Sudoku is in development and is not publicly released. This notice covers this website and describes the intended Android release. Planned integrations and privacy controls are not a statement that those services are already operating. We will verify the release build and update this notice before launch.

Who we are

Ocean Code Studio (“we”) develops Ocean Code Sudoku. For support or privacy requests, contact kamarwessam@gmail.com. This notice applies to the game and this developer website.

Game data on your device

The intended game stores saved puzzles, progress, settings, statistics, hint balances, privacy choices, and a record of Pro entitlement locally. These records support gameplay, remember your choices, and keep an already verified Pro upgrade available offline. We do not plan an app login or our own cloud-save service.

Downloaded puzzle packs are game content. Android or your device manufacturer may back up or transfer app data, depending on your device settings and the final build configuration. Local storage does not mean data can never leave your device through those system services.

Google Play purchases and downloads

We plan a one-time Pro purchase through Google Play. Google processes the payment; the game uses purchase identifiers, purchase state, and verification information to unlock or restore Pro. We do not receive your full payment card details. Buying or restoring may require an internet connection and your Google Play account.

Optional additional puzzle packs may use Google Play Asset Delivery. Google can process download and device information when providing these services. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising in the free version

We plan to use Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads and Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between games. The final participating networks will be confirmed before release.

When used, these services may process advertising or other device identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic information. Their purposes include serving and measuring ads, limiting repeated ads, and preventing fraud. Data handled depends on the installed SDKs, available permissions, your region, and privacy choices.

Pro is intended to remove all ads, including rewarded ads, with an ad-free way to obtain hints. Pro does not remove processing required for Google Play purchases, restoration, or downloads. See Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Privacy choices and diagnostics

We plan an in-app privacy control to present required choices and let you revisit them. Choosing non-personalized ads does not necessarily stop all technical data processing. Android also provides advertising identifier controls. Available options depend on your device and region.

Development builds may contain legacy Unity Analytics and crash-reporting components. We plan to disable these before release unless a revised notice explains their use. Their removal and the behavior of advertising SDKs still require release-build verification. This notice does not claim that development builds collect no analytics or diagnostics.

Intended audience

The game is intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 and over, not directed at children under 13. Regional rules can require additional protections for younger users. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us.

Support, retention, and deletion

If you email us, we receive your address and anything you include. We use this to respond and handle your request. Avoid sending sensitive information. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the conversation and relevant obligations; provider records follow the providers’ own policies.

You can remove local game records by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it. Device backups may need separate deletion and may restore data later. Removing the app does not delete Google Play purchase history or data held by advertising providers.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal information. Email us for requests about information we hold. For provider-held information, use the controls and contacts in their policies. We may need information to verify a request; do not send identity documents unless specifically necessary.

This website and future changes

These pages add no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, external fonts, or cookies. The hosting provider may process standard request information, including IP addresses and browser details, to serve and protect the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We will update this page when the app’s services or practices change and revise the date above. The release notice and Google Play Data safety information must reflect the verified app.
